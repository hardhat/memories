Memories of Ravenwood College
Dating sim

mc: Brad Bart

It is time for college, and Brad Bart couldn't be happier to have gotten into his school of choice. He is going to attend his father's alma mater, Ravenwood College.  His father even arranged to have him stay in town with his father's best friend Gord McVine, a pumpkin farmer at the edge of town.  It is time for first dates, a little pumpkin farming, and a little mystery to keep things spicy.

## The World of Ravenwood

Ravenwood is a small, fog-prone college town where the crows outnumber the students, the pumpkins grow suspiciously large, and everybody has a theory about why. The college sits at the center of town, the farms ring the edge, and the only two places anyone actually socializes are the tavern and the cafe. Underneath the autumn charm, something odd is going on with the town's pumpkins, and a certain lab on campus seems to know more than it admits (the mystery thread running through the incidents below).

## Locations

- **McVine pumpkin farm (Uncle Gord)**: Brad's home base. Equal parts farmhouse, workshop, and pumpkin cathedral.
- **Log and Stump Tavern**: The town's one tavern, run by the Stump family. Trivia night is a blood sport.
- **Ravenwood classroom, student centre, library**: Lectures, orientation, and the library's famously unreliable quiet hours.
- **Grove Cafe**: Family-run cafe with the best pastries in town and a "Pumpkin Spice Everything" menu that is not a joke.

## Characters

### Brad Bart (main character)
A fresh-faced first-year with a sunny attitude, a hand-me-down college legacy, and exactly zero farming experience. Brad grew up hearing his father's glory-days stories about Ravenwood and is determined to live up to them, or at least find out which ones were exaggerated.
*Comedic twist:* Every story his father told him about Ravenwood turns out to be slightly wrong, so Brad keeps confidently giving "local" advice that gets him laughed out of rooms.

### Gord McVine (Uncle Gord)
Brad's host and the gruff, bearded pumpkin farmer at the edge of town. He is Brad's father's best friend from their Ravenwood days, speaks mostly in grunts and farming proverbs, and is fiercely protective of his prize gourds and his daughter.
*Comedic twist:* He treats his pumpkins like family, with names, birthdays, and a memorial patch, and he has more emotional range with the pumpkins than with any human.

### Jane McVine (Gord's daughter)
Warm, redheaded, and sharp, Jane is a Ravenwood student who grew up on the farm and knows every field, shortcut, and secret in town. She's Brad's built-in guide to college life, and she does not let him forget that she's the one who actually knows what she's doing. Her formal look appears at the Halloween dance.
*Comedic twist:* She's the farm's unofficial pumpkin-sales negotiator and has never lost a haggle, even against a scarecrow she mistook for a customer.

### Sandra Stump
A goth, whip-smart student whose family owns the Log and Stump Tavern. She's dry, observant, and quietly the best-informed person in Ravenwood, since the tavern is where all the town's gossip eventually ends up. Moody on the outside, loyal underneath.
*Comedic twist:* She is dramatically gloomy about everything except tavern trivia night, where she becomes a joyful, trash-talking champion who will absolutely end friendships over a wrong answer.

### Georgia Grove
The bright, sunny blonde whose family runs the Grove Cafe. She works shifts between classes, remembers everyone's order, and treats every customer like an old friend. She's the heart of the student scene, and she's also the first to notice when something in town isn't right.
*Comedic twist:* She is an incredible baker and an incredibly *dangerous* one. Her experimental pumpkin recipes range from heavenly to hospital-visit, and she insists they're all "a work in progress."

### Prof. Patricia Peters
Brad's poised, glasses-wearing professor, respected for her brilliant lectures and feared for her pop quizzes. She is Brad's guide through the academic side of Ravenwood, and she's hiding something about what's really happening in her lab.
*Comedic twist:* She is a serious scientist who is terrible at technology. The projector, the lab equipment, and her own email defeat her daily, and she has blamed more than one catastrophe on "ravens."

## Incidents

Synopses below are placeholders to be expanded into full scripts.

1. **Moving to McVine Farm**: Brad arrives at the edge of town with too many suitcases and not enough boots. Uncle Gord puts him to work on his first day, and Brad meets Jane, who watches him lose a fight with a wheelbarrow. At dinner, Gord mentions that the pumpkins have been "acting strange this year," and Brad assumes he's joking.
2. **Orientation**: Brad tours the campus, finds the student centre, and meets Sandra and Georgia while hopelessly lost. Prof. Peters gives a welcome speech that is cut short when the projector catches fire, and a crow steals Brad's name tag, which he will later wish he'd taken more seriously.
3. **Lab Accident**: A chemistry demonstration in Prof. Peters's lab goes spectacularly wrong when a strange orange residue from a pumpkin sample reacts with something it shouldn't. Brad is stuck helping with the cleanup, and the professor is oddly eager to keep the incident quiet. The first real clue of the mystery.
4. **Homecoming**: The town celebrates homecoming with a parade, a football game, and Brad's father's old friends telling Brad contradictory stories about his dad. Brad bonds with his love interest of choice at the festivities, and someone leaves an unsigned note about the lab in his jacket.
5. **Cafe Dessert**: A study break at the Grove Cafe turns into a sweet, slightly awkward date (or group outing) built around Georgia's newest dessert experiment. The pastry is delicious and somewhat glowing. Everyone agrees not to ask why.
6. **Pumpkin Farming**: Gord teaches Brad the real work of a pumpkin farm: planting, hauling, and the proper way to talk to a gourd. One pumpkin patch is growing impossibly fast, and Brad finds the same orange residue from the lab in the soil.
7. **Midterm Library**: Midterms hit and the library becomes a battlefield of snacks, flashcards, and panic. Brad studies late with his chosen route, and a hidden section of the stacks reveals old Ravenwood records about the college's "pumpkin research program" decades ago, with his father's name in them.
8. **Pumpkin Sales**: The McVine farm sells pumpkins at the town market, and Brad is Gord's reluctant salesman. Jane's haggling steals the show, tavern and cafe friends stop by to help, and Brad realizes someone is buying pumpkins in suspicious bulk and not carving any of them.
9. **Halloween Dance**: Everyone dresses up for the Halloween dance at the college. The mystery comes to a head when the town's pumpkins act up at the worst possible moment, the truth about the lab and Brad's father is revealed, and Brad shares a final dance (and a possible confession) with his chosen route.
